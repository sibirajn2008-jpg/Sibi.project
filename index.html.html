HOW IT WORKS

From humid air to recovered water

Six stages, fully monitored, from the AC coil to controlled reuse.

STAGE 01

AC operates

Warm humid air passes over the cooling coil, condensing moisture as a natural by‑product of cooling.

STAGE 02

Condensate is collected

CONDENSA ONE intercepts the condensate line at the AC unit instead of letting it drain away.

STAGE 03

Pre‑filtration

Collected water passes through a pre‑filter stage to remove dust and particulate before storage.

STAGE 04

Separate recovery tank

Filtered water is routed into a dedicated recovery tank — physically separate from any drainage line.

STAGE 05

Level monitoring

CONDENSA SENSE tracks tank level, inflow rate and system health, visible live on the dashboard.

STAGE 06

Controlled non‑potable reuse

Recovered water is released for approved non‑potable uses such as flushing, cleaning and irrigation.

NOT CONNECTED TO SEWAGE OR WASTEWATER

Sewage / Toilet Wastewater
Normal Drainage System

CONDENSA's recovery loop is a fully separate line drawn only from AC condensate. It never intersects sewage, greywater or toilet wastewater — recovered water only ever enters the dedicated tank shown in Stage 04.
